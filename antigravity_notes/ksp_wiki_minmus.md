# Kerbal Space Program Wiki: Minmus Reference Sheet

This reference sheet contains key orbital and physical parameters for the celestial body **Minmus**, extracted from the official KSP Wiki. It should be referenced when writing or verifying maneuvers targeting Minmus.

## 1. Orbital Characteristics (Orbiting Kerbin)
*   **Semi-major axis:** $47,000,000 \text{ m}$ (from Kerbin's center)
*   **Apoapsis:** $47,000,000 \text{ m}$
*   **Periapsis:** $47,000,000 \text{ m}$
*   **Orbital Eccentricity:** $0$ (circular orbit)
*   **Orbital Inclination:** $6^\circ$
*   **Argument of Periapsis:** $38^\circ$
*   **Longitude of Ascending Node (LAN):** $78^\circ$
*   **Sidereal Orbital Period:** $1,077,311 \text{ s}$ (49 Kerbin days, 5 hours, 15 minutes, 10.5 seconds)
*   **Orbital Velocity (around Kerbin):** $\approx 274 \text{ m/s}$

## 2. Physical Characteristics
*   **Equatorial Radius:** $60,000 \text{ m}$
*   **Equatorial Circumference:** $376,991 \text{ m}$
*   **Surface Area:** $4.5238934 \times 10^{10} \text{ m}^2$
*   **Mass:** $2.6457580 \times 10^{19} \text{ kg}$
*   **Standard Gravitational Parameter ($\mu$):** $1.7658000 \times 10^9 \text{ m}^3/\text{s}^2$
*   **Surface Gravity:** $0.491 \text{ m/s}^2$ ($0.05 \text{ g}$)
*   **Escape Velocity:** $242.61 \text{ m/s}$
*   **Rotation Period:** $40,400 \text{ s}$ (1 Kerbin day, 5 hours, 13 minutes, 20 seconds)
*   **Sphere of Influence (SOI) Radius:** $2,247,428.4 \text{ m}$ (from Minmus's center)
*   **Atmosphere Present:** No

## 3. Surface & Navigation Safety Notes
*   **Terrain Extremes:** Minmus is composed of high ice plateaus and low flat plains ("Flats").
*   **Highest Peaks:** The highest peaks are over **$5.7 \text{ km}$ ($5,700 \text{ m}$)** in altitude near the poles.
*   **Minimum Safe Orbit:** Any orbit below $6 \text{ km}$ risks terrain collision. Our target polar orbit of **$20 \text{ km}$** provides a safe buffer of $14.3 \text{ km}$ above the highest terrain.
*   **Synchronous Orbit:** Possible at an altitude of **$357.94 \text{ km}$** ($357,940 \text{ m}$ from surface / $417,940 \text{ m}$ from center).
*   **Orbital Speed at 0m Surface:** $171.5 \text{ m/s}$.
